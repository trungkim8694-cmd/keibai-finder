# Blog Writing Standard (Keibai Finder)

This document defines the high-performance, Japanese-optimized writing standard for all articles in the `/blogs` section. Follow these rules strictly to ensure maximum SEO impact and premium user experience.

## 1. Visual & Layout Standards (Airy & Compact)
- **Cover Image**: Every post MUST have a high-quality featured image (1024x1024 or 16:9).
- **Separators**: Avoid using horizontal rules (`---`). Use **single blank lines** between paragraphs and **double blank lines** before new sections to create a clean vertical rhythm.
- **Lists**: Use bullet points (`-`) or numbered lists (`1.`) for clarity.
- **Bold Text**: Highlight key terms and important concepts using **bold**.
- **Emojis**: Use relevant emojis (💡, ✅, ⚠️, 🚀, 📊, 🏛️) to guide the reader's eye.

## 2. Link Standards
- **Appearance**: All links are automatically styled as **Blue, Bold, and Underlined** with an automatic arrow icon (**↗**) at the end. Do not add the arrow manually.
- **Internal Backlinks**: Every article must link to at least two of these core tools:
  - `[Bảng Phân Tích Thị Trường](/insights)` ↗
  - `[Bản Đồ Phân Tích Khu Vực](/area-map)` ↗
  - `[Tra Cứu Giá Thực Tế](/trade-find)` ↗
  - `[Tìm kiếm bất động sản](/)` ↗
- **CTA Boxes**: Place important internal links inside a `> blockquote` or a dedicated CTA paragraph with a clear leading emoji like 👉.

## 3. SEO & Content Structure
- **Primary Language**: Japanese (JA) is the primary content language. However, all slugs must follow global SEO standards (English/Romaji).
- **Slug Standards**:
  - **Format**: Only lowercase English letters, numbers, and hyphens (`-`).
  - **Language**: English or Romaji (e.g., `keibai`, `koubai`).
  - **Length**: Keep it concise (5-7 words maximum).
  - **FORBIDDEN**: Never use Japanese characters, spaces, or special symbols in the final slug.

## 4. Tone & Readability
- **Professional & Educational**: Maintain a trustworthy tone. Explain complex legal terms (like *San-ten-setto* or *Hikihodashi Meirei*) simply.
- **Justification**: The system automatically applies `text-justify: inter-character`. Write in clear, concise sentences to benefit from this alignment.
- **NO META-LABELS**: Strictly forbid using technical or planning labels in the final content (e.g., "Case Study:", "Plot:", "Context:", "Pros/Cons:", "Strategy:").
- **Natural Narrative**: Content must be written as a natural, immersive story or guide for the reader. If meta-labels were used during the drafting phase, they MUST be removed before publishing.

## 5. Technical Categories
- `GUIDE`: Educational "How-to" content, process explanations, and A-Z guides.
- `CAUTION`: Warning about risks, occupant issues, and legal pitfalls.
- `MARKET_REPORT`: (Redirected to /insights) Data-driven market analysis.
